# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-29_

Budget is built so that we never receive your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or account system, and there is no server operated by the developer.

## Where your data lives

- **On your device**, in an encrypted database.
- **In your Google Drive**, only if you turn on device sync. The files are end-to-end encrypted with a key that only your devices hold. The app uses the hidden app-data folder and cannot see your other Drive files.

## Third parties you may choose to connect

| Service | When | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| SimpleFIN Bridge | You turn on bank sync | Requests made with your SimpleFIN access token, sent from your device. Governed by SimpleFIN's own privacy policy and fees. |
| Google Drive | You turn on device sync | Encrypted files only |
| MCP clients (e.g. AI assistants) | You turn on the MCP server on macOS | Budget data you allow, with card and account numbers redacted. What the client does with it is governed by that client's policy. |

## Sensitive numbers

Do not store full card or bank account numbers in the app. If one ends up in a memo or payee anyway, the app blocks it, redacts it before any MCP client sees it, and lets you remove it permanently from history.

## Children

The app has no sign-up and collects no personal information, from anyone of any age.

## Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/Shir0o/budget/issues. Report security problems privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
